6.1800 tidbits (spring 2025)

welcome to the tidbits website! this site contains a collection of interesting things we just don't have time for in class.

have a question, or something else you'd like to see on here? email lmwilson at mit.edu!

dns

  • mess with dns, a website where you can experiment with making your own dns records!
  • dns lookup, a website where you can look at dns query output in a very readable format
  • "ICANN and the 7 keys to the internet", a really fun WVFRM podcast episode about the hunt for the domain name pizza.pizza (and DNS, ICANN, and how domain name ownership works)

unix (part 1)

  • at&t archives: the unix OS, an awesome mini-film made by bell labs in 1982 on the design of unix
  • recreation of space travel, the game made by ken thompson that somewhat influenced the design of unix. there's a link to play space travel online there, too!
  • paper by dennis ritchie on the evolution of C, starting with its origins in the unix project

unix (part 2)

  • heroes in a bash shell, a podcast episode about unix, the development of shells, and brian fox, the person behind the bash shell
  • the unix haters handbook if you want a funny, yet also informative read
  • the unix game, some interesting shell programming exercises

encapsulation

  • "the internet was designed with a narrow waist" and "a sketch of the biggest idea in software architecture", two really awesome blog posts about the power of the narrow waist idea
  • xkcd ip map of the internet
  • what would happen if we didn't use tcp or udp?, a cute experiment looking into making a custom transport protocol

resilient overlay networks

  • fixing internet real time applications, a three part blog post from riot games about how they built riot direct, to overcome BGP inadequacies

end-to-end argument

  • udp and me, a blog post by david reed on his embarrassment about udp and how its development connected to the end-to-end argument
  • arm vs x86, a nice article on the difference between the two types of processors

content delivery networks

  • aws data center latencies, a fun visualization of latencies between amazon's data centers (shows why we can benefit from servers close to users!)
  • the canva outage: another tale of saturation and resilience, a blog post about how an issue with cloudflare's cdn caused a canva outage
  • netflix's open connect, a less technical article from the verge about netflix's content delivery network

physical deployability

  • so you want to build your own data center, a fascinating technical blog post by railway about their migration off of google cloud into a colocation data center
  • dropbox magic pocket, introductory blog post in a series about dropbox's magic pocket storage

google file system

  • interview with google engineers on the design of gfs, a cool and somewhat different look into the development of gfs

zettabyte file system

  • ZFS: the other new apple file system that almost was—until it wasn’t , the story of apple's almost-relationship with ZFS
